How it works

Simple, safe and private.

  1. Search
    Pick the blood group needed. We apply the standard compatibility rules for you.
  2. Request
    Send a contact request to a donor. They see your name, phone and urgency.
  3. Connect
    If the donor accepts, their phone and email are shown to you.
